## Key findings that shape the course

- The current standards are the **Georgia K-12 Mathematics Standards adopted in 2021**, implemented in high school in 2023-24. Course name: Algebra: Concepts & Connections.
- GaDOE's map has 9 units. The block (one-semester) pacing totals 68-94 days, which matches an 18-week semester of about 90 days.
- Standards are organized by big ideas: MP, MM, NR, PAR, FGR, GSR, DSR. A.MM.1 and A.MP.1-8 run through every unit.
- Scope limits from the Evidence of Student Learning that the content respects:
  - A.NR.5.1: operations with square roots and cube roots are limited to addition, subtraction and multiplication.
  - A.PAR.6.2: polynomial operations limited to degree 2 or less (monomial x binomial, binomial x binomial).
  - A.PAR.6.3: solve by inspection, square roots, factoring, completing the square, quadratic formula; **real solutions only**.
  - A.PAR.8.2: exponential equations limited to like bases or bases easily rewritten as like bases.
  - A.FGR.2.3: both interval and set-builder notation are expected.
  - A.FGR.9.3: exponential transformations limited to f(x) + k and k f(x).
  - A.DSR.10.2: the 1.5 x IQR rule identifies outliers.
  - A.DSR.10.5: the line of best fit and r are calculated with technology; the app supplies the calculation tool.
